# Difficulty rubric

1. A one-place change: copy, config, a constant, an obvious fix.
2. A small change in one module, with a test.
3. A change across a few modules, or a new behaviour with its tests.
4. A cross-cutting change, a migration, or one with tricky edge cases or concurrency.
5. Design work: new architecture, unclear trade-offs, or high blast radius.

Confidence is how sure you are of the score, 0 to 1.
